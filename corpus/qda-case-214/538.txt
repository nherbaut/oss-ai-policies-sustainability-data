# About Flathub: The Linux App Store

## Whether you're a user looking for apps or a developer looking to reach more users, Flathub is the best choice for apps on Linux.

## Users

All the apps you want — From big names you'd expect to fresh indie developers, Flathub has thousands of apps to meet your needs.

Transparent safety — Clearly see when an app is [verified](https://docs.flathub.org/docs/for-users/verification/) as coming from its developer, what permissions it requires, and whether or not it's open source and auditable

Tastefully curated — Discover interesting, quality apps across Flathub with our staff curation that showcases the best on offer

Apps for you where you are — Whether you're on a Steam Deck, a powerful Linux workstation, a Raspberry Pi, or the rare Linux phone; Flathub has apps for you

## Developers

Reach millions of users — Flathub comes out of the box on multiple Linux distros, is [easily installable](/en/setup) on the rest, and has over a million active users

Independent & transparent — We're a grassroots open source community stewarding the best of what the ecosystem has to offer

Clear docs & guidance — Extensive [documentation](https://docs.flathub.org/docs/category/for-app-authors), thousands of public [app manifests](https://github.com/flathub/), and a large community means you're always able to get help

Native app store integration — Forget web downloads; Flathub delivers apps and [automatic updates](https://docs.flathub.org/docs/for-app-authors/updates) to users in their native app store client where they'd expect
